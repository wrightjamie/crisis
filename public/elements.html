Typography & Text Scales
Font size and weight tokens from tokens.css and utility classes from style.css.
Type Sizes & Weights
.text-xs (0.65rem) - Secondary timestamp or meta tag
.text-sm (0.75rem) - Button labels and form instructions
.text-base (0.90rem) - Body text and card descriptions
.text-md (1.10rem) - Panel headers and subheadings
.text-lg (1.50rem) - Screen titles and hero numbers
