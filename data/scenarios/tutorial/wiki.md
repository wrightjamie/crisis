# Tutorial Scenario Wiki

Welcome to the Tutorial Scenario. This wiki provides basic information for the scenario.

## Core Concepts
- **Scores**: Watch your scores closely. Some decisions will increase or decrease them.
- **Timed Events**: Some events will trigger automatically after a certain delay.
- **Roles**: Each role has specific tasks and information.
- **Assets**: Assets on the map can change state and affect conditions for future decisions.

## Local Emergency Protocol
In the event of a local emergency, the EOC (Emergency Operations Center) coordinates the response. Always prioritize saving lives over property.
