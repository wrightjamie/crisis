UK Crisis: Russian Tensions — Facilitator Guide

Scenario Overview: A multi-ministerial Cabinet Office Briefing Room (COBRA) crisis simulation for 2–6 players (PM mandatory; optional home, defence, foreign, media, cyber with automatic roleFallbacks). Spanning 74 events and 12 manual actions across 5 escalation stages, this scenario combines facilitator-paced chapter progression (unlockEvents) with probabilistic hybrid-warfare twists (randomEvents), Eyes-Only intelligence, timed COBRA injects, and auto-triggered national shocks.

Facilitator Pacing Model (You Control the Tempo):
• Opening (ev_kremlin_ultimatum): A televised Kremlin statement condemns European & UK intervention in Ukraine as crossing a red line, placing Russian fleets on alert. COBRA's diplomatic & readiness posture unlocks early Stage 1 crises (including UK Casualty Leak) and rolls probabilistic hybrid-warfare aftershocks.
• Stage 1 → 2 (Grey Zone & Sabotage): Protests, maritime cable shadowing, Salisbury nerve-agent alert, bank cyber-attacks, NHS ransomware, North Sea pipeline sabotage, and—if UK–US ties drop (≤ 2)—a White House Non-Support Statement that withdraws & hides US assets from theatre.
• Stage 3 → 4 (Clashes & Kinetic Escalation): Faslane submarine contact, Black Sea/Atlantic near-misses, RAF Akrotiri & UK base strikes, National Grid blackouts, and Kaliningrad Iskander dispersal.
• Stage 5 (Climax & Final COBRA Vote): Article 5 ambiguity, US neutrality risk, secret Moscow backchannels → ev_final_authorization → 3 mutually exclusive Endgames.

Pre-Game Variant Setup (4 Geopolitical Axes)

  • HMS Prince of Wales Location: Deployed to RIMPAC (Australia) (-1 Readiness; carrier 3 weeks away) vs English Channel (+1 Readiness; carrier ready in hours).

  • Domestic Climate: Stable Economy (+1 Stability) vs Winter of Discontent (-2 Stability; immediate strike/panic-buying vulnerability).

COBRA CABINET PLAYER BRIEFING & REFERENCE PACK — ISSUE TO PLAYERS

UK Crisis: Russian Tensions — COBRA Cabinet Brief

Situation: Early 2025. NATO–Russia tensions have reached breaking point following Russian force buildups along the Baltic frontier. The Prime Minister has convened an emergency COBRA (Cabinet Office Briefing Rooms) session. Expect hybrid warfare across cyber, undersea infrastructure, disinformation, domestic unrest, maritime standoffs, and potential kinetic strikes.

COBRA Ministerial Stations (2–6 Players)

Ministerial DeskCore Responsibilities
Prime Minister (PM) (Mandatory)Chairs COBRA, resolves cross-departmental trade-offs, holds ultimate authority over national strategy, deep-strike options, and the final war-or-ceasefire vote.
Home Secretary (home)Internal security, policing, counter-sabotage (MI5), Civil Contingencies powers, and keeping streets calm without eroding civil liberties.
Defence Secretary (defence)Commands Royal Navy, RAF, and British Army deployments, QRA air intercepts, anti-submarine defence of Faslane, and kinetic Rules of Engagement.
Foreign Secretary (foreign)Lead diplomat managing NATO cohesion, the UK–US Special Relationship, European defence partners, sanctions, and backchannel talks with Moscow.
Media & Comms Director (media)Manages Downing Street press strategy, counters hostile disinformation and deepfakes, and prevents public panic or market runs.
Cyber Security Lead (cyber)Directs NCSC and GCHQ cyber defence of banks, NHS trusts, telecoms, and the National Grid—plus covert offensive cyber options.

Key UK & Allied Strategic Assets on the Tactical Map

  • HMNB Clyde (Faslane) & Vanguard-Class Submarines: Home of the UK's Continuous At-Sea Nuclear Deterrent (CASD).
  • GCHQ Bude & National Grid Control: Critical signals intelligence and national energy/electricity transmission hubs.
  • RAF Combat & Lift Fleet: Typhoon FGR4 (QRA), F-35B Lightning II (stealth strike), Voyager tankers, and A400M Atlas.
  • Royal Navy Fleet: Carrier Strike Group (HMS Prince of Wales), Type 45 Destroyers, Type 23/26 ASW Frigates, and Astute-class hunter-killer submarines.
  • US Allied Forces in Theatre: USAF 48th Fighter Wing (RAF Lakenheath) and the US 6th Fleet Carrier Group (North Atlantic)—available only while UK–US relations hold!

COBRA National Status Metrics (1 = Critical → 5 = Secure)

  • UK–Russia Relations & Military Escalation: Measures diplomatic headroom vs proximity to full-scale war (hitting Escalation 5 triggers NATO rapid response).
  • Civilian Stability: Domestic public order and morale (dropping to 1 triggers nationwide fuel/food panic).
  • UK–US Alliance & European Cohesion: Strength of Washington & NATO/EU backing (UK–US dropping to 1 throttles Five Eyes intelligence).
  • Military Readiness: Combat availability and resilience of UK Armed Forces.
COBRA Operating Rules:
1. Talk Across the Table: Some classified MI5/GCHQ flashes and diplomatic offers are Eyes-Only—only specific desks see them on screen. Share verbally when needed!
2. Cabinet Sign-Offs: High-risk proactive initiatives in your Actions menu (cyber strikes, MI5 raids, F-35 strikes, national addresses) require formal approval from another Minister.
3. Beat the Clock: Urgent COBRA flashes (airspace incursions, grid attacks, deepfake broadcasts, chemical alerts) have live countdown timers—indecision fires default standing orders.
