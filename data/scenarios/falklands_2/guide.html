Falklands II: Southern Storm — Facilitator Guide

Scenario Overview: A fast-paced South Atlantic military and diplomatic crisis built for solo command (minUsers: 1) or a 2–3 player command team (uk_commander + optional air_maritime and downing_street, both falling back seamlessly to uk_commander in solo play). Across 62 events and 6 manual actions, mutually exclusive probability forks (randomEvents) ensure ~55–65% of the pool fires in any single 45–60 minute run—so no two playthroughs unfold the same way.

Cascading Wave Flow (Automated Progression):
• Opening (ev_start — Timed 60s): Shock Stanley election result. Player posture sets how fast Wave 1 arrives (55s–120s).
• Wave 1 — Maritime & Air Exclusion Zone (ev_military_incursion): Launches Naval Skirmish + a mutually exclusive sub/tanker fork (ARA Salta Detected 55% vs British Tanker Seized 35%) + 65% chance of Eyes-Only Chilean Radar Backchannel Tip-Off. Naval Skirmish branches into Air Raid on MPA, Pebble Island BVR, Radar Hit, or HMS Defender Damaged → resolving to Sea Control Won/Lost.
• Wave 2 — Island Defence: Cascades into Heliborne Assault on Port Stanley or San Carlos Bay Night Landing, branching through Urban Combat, Stalemate at Stanley, or Goose Green Cut Off, alongside probabilistic wildcards (FIDF Resistance, Timed Cyber Attack, Southern Ocean Gale, Buzos Tácticos Sabotage, South Georgia Distress).
• Wave 3 — Logistics, Diplomacy & Endgame: Cascades into Critical Supply Shortage & PM Demands Decisive Action → UN Resolution or Mainland Escalation Debate (with probabilistic Casualty Leak / Regional Airspace Ban) → ev_endgame_trigger → 3 formal Endgames.

Pre-Game Variant Setup (3 Operational Axes)

  • Ascension Island Status: Fully Operational (+1 Supply) vs Sabotaged / Out of Use (-2 Supply, -1 Readiness; sets ascension_island to sabotaged, locking out A400M airlift & reinforcements until act_ascension_sealift restores it).
  • Weather Conditions: Clear Skies (+1 Readiness) vs Severe Storms (-1 Readiness, -1 Supply).
  • Early Warning Intel: Surprise Attack (+1 Aggression, +1 Pressure, HMS Defender further east) vs Advance Warning (+1 Readiness, +1 Public Support).

Falklands II: Southern Storm — Operations Brief

Situation: A fiercely contested local election in Port Stanley has produced a shock pro-Argentine Mayor-elect amid MI6 suspicions of foreign interference. Argentine naval, air, and covert units are massing along the edge of the Falklands Total Exclusion Zone. Your mission is to defend British sovereign territory, protect island civilians, keep the 7,700-mile Ascension Island supply bridge open, and manage intense political pressure from Downing Street and the UN.

Command Structure (Solo or 2–3 Player Team)

StationCore Responsibilities
UK Task Force Commander (Mandatory)Overall theatre command across land, sea, and air; ground defence of Port Stanley & MPA; coordinating recon, logistics, and final strategic assessment. (Handles all desks seamlessly in solo play.)
Air & Maritime Ops (Optional)1435 Flight Typhoons (QRA/BVR), HMS Defender (Type 45 air defence), ASW submarine hunting, 1564 Flight helicopters, and A400M/Voyager AAR tasking.
Downing Street & Foreign Office (Optional)UK public support, press/media handling, UN & allied diplomacy, discreet intelligence backchannels, and political approval for mainland strikes or SAS operations.

Theatre Metrics (1 = Critical → 5 = Optimal)

  • UK Public Support: Domestic backing in Britain. Falling to 1 risks an ultimatum from Downing Street.
  • Military Readiness: Combat effectiveness of BFSAI air, naval, and land forces. Must be 3+ for Victory.
  • International Pressure: Diplomatic heat from the UN and regional states (lower is better; hitting 5 triggers an emergency ceasefire ultimatum, while keeping it ≤ 2 may unlock discreet US support).
  • Argentine Aggression: Tempo of hostile offensive action (hitting 5 triggers a saturation air raid).
  • Supply Lines: Health of the Ascension air/sea logistics bridge (dropping to 1 risks a Wideawake fuel crisis).
Command Tips:
1. Hunt & Finish Hidden Threats: Intelligence and recon sweeps reveal concealed enemy units on the map—if a submarine or coastal missile launcher slips away intact, it can return later to ambush your ships.
2. Use Your Actions Menu: Proactive operations (Recon Sweeps, HMS Astute Patrols, Ascension Fuel Sealift, Reinforcements, and Mainland Options) unlock dynamically as the crisis develops.
3. Watch the Clocks: High-tempo air raids, submarine contacts, and cyber attacks carry live countdown timers—indecision triggers default standing orders.
