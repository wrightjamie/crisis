# Crisis Wargame Image Style Guide

To maintain visual cohesion across the Crisis Wargame platform, all generated or sourced imagery should adhere to the following stylistic guidelines.

## The Core Aesthetic: "Stormy Tactical"

The game relies on a serious, high-stakes military and intelligence atmosphere. Images should evoke tension, realism, and operational drama. 

### Key Elements
1.  **High Realism**: Images should look like professional military photography or high-end simulations, not cartoons or simple digital art. Use terms like `highly realistic`, `dramatic military photograph`, `unreal engine 5`, and `4k`.
2.  **Atmosphere**: The environment should feel imposing. For outdoor scenes, use `stormy sky`, `stormy sea`, `rough ocean waves`, or `overcast`. For indoor scenes, use `low lighting`, `glowing screens`, and `tense atmosphere`.
3.  **Color Palette**: Stick to cool, muted tones: dark blues, greys, and blacks, accented by harsh artificial lighting (e.g., neon blue tactical screens, harsh white spotlights, or emergency red lighting).
4.  **Composition**: The subject (whether an asset, a riot, or an alien ship) should be the undeniable focus, framed centrally with dramatic perspective.

### Prompt Formulas for AI Generation

When generating new images using an AI tool, use the following base formulas depending on the subject matter:

#### Military Assets (Naval/Air)
> "A highly realistic, dramatic military photograph of a [NATION/FACTION] [SPECIFIC ASSET NAME] [ACTION/ENVIRONMENT], stormy [sea/sky], high quality, 4k, tactical aesthetic"
> *Example*: "A highly realistic, dramatic military photograph of a UK Royal Navy Type 45 Daring-class destroyer cutting through rough ocean waves, stormy sea, high quality, 4k, tactical aesthetic"

#### Civilian / Urban Crises
> "A highly realistic, dramatic photograph of [CRISIS EVENT] in a major city, overcast weather, tense atmosphere, high quality, 4k, news report aesthetic"
> *Example*: "A highly realistic, dramatic photograph of mass anti-war protests with riot police in Manchester city centre, overcast weather, tense atmosphere, high quality, 4k, news report aesthetic"

#### Intelligence / Cyber Operations
> "A highly realistic, dramatic photograph of a [LOCATION/ROOM TYPE], dark room with glowing blue tactical screens, tense atmosphere, high quality, 4k, military intelligence aesthetic"
> *Example*: "A highly realistic, dramatic photograph of a UK Cyber Security Operations Center, dark room with glowing blue tactical screens, tense atmosphere, high quality, 4k, military intelligence aesthetic"

#### Alien / Sci-Fi Threats
> "A highly realistic, terrifying, dramatic photograph of [SCIFI THREAT DESCRIPTION], apocalyptic stormy sky, high quality, 4k, dark sci-fi military aesthetic"
> *Example*: "A highly realistic, terrifying, dramatic photograph of a massive 500km alien mothership hovering in orbit above Earth, apocalyptic stormy sky, high quality, 4k, dark sci-fi military aesthetic"

## Best Practices
- **Avoid Text**: AI generators struggle with text. Try to frame scenes so that specific text (like banners, news chyrons, or tail numbers) isn't the focal point, or omit requests for specific text entirely.
- **Aspect Ratio**: The platform generally prefers landscape (16:9) or wide aspect ratios for cards and briefings.
- **Consistency**: If a faction's color scheme is established (e.g., green for aliens, blue for NATO), ensure prompts reinforce this.
